Traveliary

Mobile app · iOS & Android · Private beta

A travel diary in photos. You take a photo where you are, it lands on a map, and the next traveler can see who has been there before them. Includes "Must see": the best-known places within 50 km, anywhere in the world.

  • In-app camera and photo editor (crop, filters, adjustments) rendered on the GPU with Skia
  • Offline-first posting with a local upload queue; push notifications
  • Privacy by design: exact locations are never shown, and there's no sign-up
  • Postgres row-level security, PostGIS spatial queries, Edge Functions; 90+ unit tests
  • Built with AI pair-programming (Claude Code), from idea to beta
  • React Native
  • Expo
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • PostGIS
  • Skia
